**Obsidian Cup Media Consent — version 1.0**

**A. Event coverage (required to take part)**

Obsidian Cup events are photographed, filmed and may be live-streamed. By taking part, you agree that:

1. Obsidian Cup and its appointed photographers and videographers may photograph, film and record you during the event, including warm-ups, matches, award ceremonies and around the venue.
2. These photos, videos and recordings, together with your name, team and results, may be used to document the event and to promote Obsidian Cup on our website, social media (including Instagram and Facebook), printed material and in reports to partners and the Bangladesh Volleyball Federation or other governing bodies.
3. You will not be paid for this use. Obsidian Cup owns the photos and videos it takes.
4. Material may be edited, cropped or combined with other material, but never in a way that is misleading, offensive or harmful to your reputation.
5. Partners and sponsors of an event may share our posts that include you, but may not use your image in their own advertising without asking you separately.

**B. Individual feature (your choice)**

Tick if you agree. You can change this at any time in your profile.

- [ ] I agree that Obsidian Cup may feature me individually in promotional posts, such as player spotlights, highlight clips centred on me, and posters.

If you do not tick B, you may still appear in team photos, crowd shots and match footage, but we will not make posts that focus on you alone.

**Removal requests**

If you are unhappy with a specific photo or post, email obsidiancup.official@gmail.com with a link. We will review it within 7 days and remove it from our own channels if there is a reasonable concern. We cannot remove copies already shared by others, or printed material already distributed.

**Under 18**

For players under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to part A and choose part B on the player's behalf.
