**Obsidian Cup Payment, Refund and Event Change Policy — version 1.0**

**Paying**

1. The fee, late fee, deadline and bKash number for each event are shown on the event page. The fee is per team.
2. Submitting a registration does not reserve a slot. A slot is confirmed only when Obsidian Cup has received and verified the payment and issued a confirmation.
3. The team must pay within 48 hours of submitting its registration, or the registration may be cancelled and the slot given to another team.
4. The captain enters the bKash transaction ID in the team's registration. Each transaction ID can be used for one team only.
5. **(new)** Payment must come from a bKash number linked to the team's captain or vice-captain, so refunds can be returned safely.
6. We confirm verified payments within 3 days.
7. Slots are given in order of verified payment. When a category is full, further teams join a waitlist and are refunded in full if no slot opens.

**If your team withdraws**

| When the captain cancels in writing | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| At least 24 hours before the event starts | 50% of the fee |
| Less than 24 hours before, or not showing up | No refund |
| Team disqualified for false information or misconduct | No refund |

Cancellations must be emailed by the captain to obsidiancup.official@gmail.com.

**If Obsidian Cup changes the event**

| What happens | Your options |
| --- | --- |
| Event cancelled and not rescheduled | Full refund |
| **(new)** Date, venue or format changed by us | Keep your slot for the new date or venue, or withdraw within 7 days of our notice for a full refund |
| **(new)** Start time changed on the same day, or a match moved to another court at the same venue | No refund; the event continues as planned |
| **(new)** Event stopped after it has started, for safety, weather, government orders, unrest or other reasons outside our control | The tournament committee decides results from matches played. If fewer than half of the scheduled matches were played and the event is not completed later, teams receive a 50% refund |
| **(new)** Your team cannot reach the venue because of a general strike, hartal or government restriction on the event day | Treated the same as the event being stopped, if we postpone; otherwise no refund |

**How refunds are paid**

1. **(new)** Refunds are sent by bKash to the number the payment came from, within 14 days of being approved.
2. **(new)** bKash charges for sending the payment are not refunded.
3. Refunds go to the team, not to individual players. Sharing a refund among players is the captain's responsibility.

**Changes we tell you about**

We announce changes by email to captains and players, in the website account, and on our official Instagram @obsidiancup and Facebook page. Captains must make sure their players know.
