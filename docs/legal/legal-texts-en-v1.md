# Obsidian Cup — Player Accounts: Legal & Consent Texts (English source)

Version 1.0 · updated 31 Oct 2026 · English source

## How to use this doc

This is the English source for every legal text players will see in the Obsidian Cup account system. Translate each section into Bangla; both versions will be shown in the app, and every agreement a player gives is saved with the document name, version, language and time.

- **Placeholders** are in square brackets, for example \[Organiser legal name\]. Fill these before launch.
- **Version:** all texts below are version 1.0. When a text changes, its version number goes up and players are asked to agree again.
- **Controlling language:** these drafts say the English version applies if the two versions differ. Decide this with your lawyer; some organisers make the Bangla version controlling for local participants.
- **Lawyer review:** these texts are written to be thorough, but I am not a lawyer. Have a Bangladeshi lawyer review them once before launch, especially the liability waiver (Event Participation Agreement, part 6) and the guardian consent. A one-time review is the single best step toward making them hold up.

**Open questions for you:**

- Organiser: Obsidian Cup is not yet a legal entity, so the texts name Uriana Faiha, trading as Obsidian Cup. Update this if a trade licence or company is registered (see the Season 5 Launch Plan, section 1).
- Postal address: none. The organiser has no postal address; email is the contact for formal notices (decided 2 Nov 2026).
- Is 30 days after each event the right time to delete ID photos?

## 1. Terms of Use (player accounts)

Shown once at sign-up. A player must agree before creating a profile.

**Obsidian Cup Account Terms of Use — version 1.1**

1. **Who we are.** Obsidian Cup is a sports tournament organiser based in Bangladesh, run by its founder and head organiser, Uriana Faiha, trading as Obsidian Cup ("Obsidian Cup", "we", "us"). These terms apply to your Obsidian Cup account and to your use of obsidiancup.vercel.app and any domain we later use.
2. **Your account.** You sign in with your own Google account, or with your own email address and a password you choose. Keep your password private; Obsidian Cup will never ask for it. You may hold only one Obsidian Cup account. You must not create an account for someone else, share your account, or let anyone else use it.
3. **Accurate information.** You must give true, complete and current information, including your full name, date of birth and contact details. You must update it if it changes. Giving false information, including a false date of birth or another person's identity, may lead to your account being suspended and to disqualification from any event, without refund.
4. **Age.** You may create an account yourself from age 13. Under 13, your account can be used only after your parent or legal guardian has given consent. If you are 13 to 17, you can create and use your account, but you cannot join a team for a tournament, upload ID documents or take part in any event until your parent or legal guardian has given consent through the guardian consent process.
5. **Captains.** Only a person given a valid captain code by Obsidian Cup may create a team. A captain code is personal, works once, and is tied to the account that uses it first.
6. **Acceptable use.** You must not: misuse the website or try to access data that is not yours; try to get around limits, rules or security; upload false, offensive or someone else's documents; harass other players or our staff; or use the platform for any unlawful purpose.
7. **Our rights.** We may refuse, suspend or close an account, remove a player from a team, or cancel a registration if these terms, the tournament rules or the law are broken. Where reasonable, we will tell you why.
8. **Availability.** We aim to keep the website working, but it may sometimes be unavailable. Registration deadlines are not extended because of a temporary outage unless we announce an extension.
9. **Closing your account.** You may ask us to close your account at any time by emailing obsidiancup.official@gmail.com. Some records may be kept for the periods set out in our Privacy Notice.
10. **Changes to these terms.** We may update these terms. If a change is important, you will be asked to agree to the new version the next time you sign in. If you do not agree, you may close your account.
11. **Law.** These terms are governed by the laws of Bangladesh. Any dispute will be handled by the courts of Dhaka, Bangladesh, after first trying to settle it with us in good faith.
12. **Contact.** obsidiancup.official@gmail.com. Formal notices to us must be sent to this address.

## 2. Privacy Notice

Shown at sign-up, and linked in the footer of every page. Written to cover what Bangladesh's [Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025](https://www.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/bangladeshs-personal-data-protection-ordinance-2025-key-takeaways-4015401) asks for: why data is collected, how it is used, who sees it, how long it is kept, and the right to withdraw consent.

**Obsidian Cup Privacy Notice — version 1.3**

**What we collect**

| Data | Required? | Why we need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email (from Google sign-in or the email you sign up with), your player code | Yes | To create and secure your account |
| Full legal name, date of birth, gender, phone number | Yes | To check eligibility for categories and age limits, and to contact you about events |
| School, college or club | For school and age-group categories | To check eligibility |
| Emergency contact: name, relationship, phone | Yes, to play in an event | To reach someone if you are injured or unwell at an event |
| Parent or guardian: name, relationship, phone, email | Only if you are under 18 | To get and record their consent |
| Photo of your ID document | Yes, to play in an event | To confirm you are the person who registered |
| Medical notes, such as allergies or conditions | Optional | To help first aid staff if you need care at an event |
| Instagram handle | Optional | To tag you in event posts, only if you choose |
| Sports you play, and a sports profile: height, weight, standing reach, jump height, playing hand and foot, year you started, preferred jersey number, shirt size, a short bio. You choose your playing level once; after that, only organisers can change it | Sports: yes. The rest: optional | To build your player profile, help organisers balance teams and categories, and keep sports records over the seasons |
| Profile photo | Optional | Shown on your player card so organisers can recognise you when they check you in at an event. We crop it to a square and make it small before saving |
| Team, position, jersey number, results | Yes, to play | To run the tournament and keep records of results |
| bKash transaction ID | Captains only | To confirm the team's payment |
| Records of what you agreed to, with date and time | Yes | To prove consent was given |

**Who can see your data**

- **You** can see and edit your own profile.
- **Your team captain** can see your name, position, jersey number and whether your registration is complete. Captains cannot see your phone number, date of birth, ID photo, emergency contact or medical notes.
- **Obsidian Cup organisers** can see everything, only to run events and keep them safe. At an event, organisers may ask to see the player card in your account, which shows your name, photo, player code, date of birth, whether your ID has been checked, and your team.
- **The public** can see team names, rosters (player names only), results and event photos. If you switch on "show my stats", your public player page also shows your height, reach, jump, playing hand and foot, level and year you started. Your weight is never shown publicly.
- **First aid or medical staff** at an event may be told your emergency contact and medical notes if you need care.
- **Authorities**, only if the law requires it.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising, and we never use it to make automated decisions about you. Players under 13 cannot add a sports profile or a profile photo until a parent or guardian has given consent.

**Where it is stored**

Your data is stored with our service provider Supabase, on servers in Singapore, and our website is hosted by Vercel. Both use encryption and access controls. By creating an account, you agree to your data being stored outside Bangladesh with these providers.

**How long we keep it**

| Data | Kept until |
| --- | --- |
| ID photos | Deleted 30 days after the event you registered for. We keep only a note that your ID was checked |
| Medical notes | Deleted 30 days after each event, unless you choose to keep them on your profile |
| Profile, sports profile, profile photo and contact details | Until you close your account, or 3 years after your last event. You can clear any sports detail or remove your photo yourself at any time |
| Results, team names and rosters | Kept permanently as part of the tournament history |
| Payment records and consent records | 6 years, to meet legal and accounting needs |

**Your rights**

You can: see the data we hold about you; correct it; download a copy; withdraw a consent you gave; and ask us to delete your data. Email obsidiancup.official@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you withdraw consent needed to take part, such as the ID check, you cannot play in upcoming events, but past results stay in the tournament history.

**If something goes wrong**

If a data breach could affect you, we will report it to the authorities as the law requires and tell affected players as soon as we can.

**Complaints**

Contact us first. You may also complain to Bangladesh's data protection authority.

**Contact:** obsidiancup.official@gmail.com

## 3. Media and Photo Consent

Shown at sign-up and again with each event. It has two parts: part A is required to play, because cameras cover the whole court and crowd. Part B is a free choice, so the consent counts as freely given.

**Obsidian Cup Media Consent — version 1.0**

**A. Event coverage (required to take part)**

Obsidian Cup events are photographed, filmed and may be live-streamed. By taking part, you agree that:

1. Obsidian Cup and its appointed photographers and videographers may photograph, film and record you during the event, including warm-ups, matches, award ceremonies and around the venue.
2. These photos, videos and recordings, together with your name, team and results, may be used to document the event and to promote Obsidian Cup on our website, social media (including Instagram and Facebook), printed material and in reports to partners and the Bangladesh Volleyball Federation or other governing bodies.
3. You will not be paid for this use. Obsidian Cup owns the photos and videos it takes.
4. Material may be edited, cropped or combined with other material, but never in a way that is misleading, offensive or harmful to your reputation.
5. Partners and sponsors of an event may share our posts that include you, but may not use your image in their own advertising without asking you separately.

**B. Individual feature (your choice)**

Tick if you agree. You can change this at any time in your profile.

- [ ] I agree that Obsidian Cup may feature me individually in promotional posts, such as player spotlights, highlight clips centred on me, and posters.

If you do not tick B, you may still appear in team photos, crowd shots and match footage, but we will not make posts that focus on you alone.

**Removal requests**

If you are unhappy with a specific photo or post, email obsidiancup.official@gmail.com with a link. We will review it within 7 days and remove it from our own channels if there is a reasonable concern. We cannot remove copies already shared by others, or printed material already distributed.

**Under 18**

For players under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to part A and choose part B on the player's behalf.

## 4. Event Participation Agreement

Every player agrees to this personally, once per event, before their team's registration can be completed. The event's own rules (category, eligibility, squad requirements, code of conduct) are shown with it and form part of it.

**Obsidian Cup Event Participation Agreement — version 1.0**

This agreement is between you and Uriana Faiha, trading as Obsidian Cup ("Obsidian Cup") for the event named on your registration ("the Event").

**1. Eligibility and honesty**

1. I meet every eligibility requirement for my category, including age, school enrolment, gender category and level of play, as stated in the Event rules.
2. I am not ineligible under the Event rules. For example, I have not played for the Bangladesh senior national team, and I do not represent any defence services or paramilitary sports team, where the Event rules exclude these.
3. All information and documents I have given are true and belong to me.
4. I understand that Obsidian Cup may check my identity and eligibility at any time before or during the Event, and may ask me to show my original ID at the venue.
5. If any information is false or misleading, I and my team may be disqualified immediately, results may be cancelled, and no fee will be refunded.

**2. Rules and conduct**

1. I will follow the Event rules, the official FIVB rules (or other rules named for the sport) unless the organisers state otherwise, the venue's rules, and the instructions of officials and organisers.
2. Decisions of match officials and the tournament committee are final.
3. I will not use abusive or threatening language or behaviour, fight, intimidate, cheat, take part in match fixing or betting on Obsidian Cup matches, smoke or vape, use alcohol or controlled substances, or damage property, at or around the venue.
4. Breaking these rules may lead to removal from the venue, suspension, disqualification of me or my team, a ban from future Obsidian Cup events, and no refund.

**3. Health and fitness**

1. I am physically fit to take part in competitive sport, and I know of no medical reason why I should not.
2. I will tell the organisers before the Event about any condition that could affect my safety, and I will stop playing if I feel unwell or injured.
3. I will not play under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or while injured against medical advice.

**4. Emergency medical care**

1. If I am injured or unwell, I agree that Obsidian Cup staff and first aid providers may give me first aid.
2. If I need further care and cannot decide for myself, and my emergency contact or guardian cannot be reached in time, I agree that the organisers may arrange transport to a hospital and that doctors may give emergency treatment they judge necessary.
3. I am responsible for the cost of any medical treatment beyond first aid at the venue.

**5. Risks**

I understand that volleyball, football and other sports involve risk of injury, including sprains, fractures, head injuries, and in rare cases serious injury. These risks can come from my own actions, other players, equipment, the playing surface, heat and weather. I choose to take part knowing these risks.

**6. Liability**

1. To the fullest extent allowed by the law of Bangladesh, Obsidian Cup, its organisers, volunteers, partners and venue hosts are not responsible for injury, illness, loss or damage that I suffer during the Event, except where it is caused by their negligence or deliberate wrongdoing.
2. Obsidian Cup is not responsible for loss of, theft of, or damage to personal belongings at the venue.
3. I am responsible for any damage I deliberately or carelessly cause to the venue, equipment or other people's property.
4. Nothing in this agreement limits any right I have that cannot be limited by law.

**7. Changes to the Event**

I understand that the Event date, time, venue, schedule, format or number of teams may change because of venue availability, weather, safety, government orders, public unrest, strikes or hartals, or other reasons outside the organisers' control. Obsidian Cup will tell registered players and captains as early as possible by email and on its official social media. What happens to fees in each case is set out in the Payment, Refund and Event Change Policy.

**8. Media**

I have read and agree to part A of the Media Consent.

**9. Data**

I have read the Privacy Notice and agree to my data being used as it describes for this Event.

**10. This agreement**

This agreement is governed by the laws of Bangladesh, and disputes will be handled by the courts of Dhaka. If one part is found invalid, the rest still applies. My agreement is recorded electronically with the date, time and version, and counts as my signature.

## 5. Payment, Refund and Event Change Policy

The captain agrees to this when registering a team; it is also linked from every event page. Rules marked **(new)** are additions to your current policy for you to confirm.

**Obsidian Cup Payment, Refund and Event Change Policy — version 1.0**

**Paying**

1. The fee, late fee, deadline and bKash number for each event are shown on the event page. The fee is per team.
2. Submitting a registration does not reserve a slot. A slot is confirmed only when Obsidian Cup has received and verified the payment and issued a confirmation.
3. The team must pay within 48 hours of submitting its registration, or the registration may be cancelled and the slot given to another team.
4. The captain enters the bKash transaction ID in the team's registration. Each transaction ID can be used for one team only.
5. **(new)** Payment must come from a bKash number linked to the team's captain or vice-captain, so refunds can be returned safely.
6. We confirm verified payments within 3 days.
7. Slots are given in order of verified payment. When a category is full, further teams join a waitlist and are refunded in full if no slot opens.

**If your team withdraws**

| When the captain cancels in writing | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| At least 24 hours before the event starts | 50% of the fee |
| Less than 24 hours before, or not showing up | No refund |
| Team disqualified for false information or misconduct | No refund |

Cancellations must be emailed by the captain to obsidiancup.official@gmail.com.

**If Obsidian Cup changes the event**

| What happens | Your options |
| --- | --- |
| Event cancelled and not rescheduled | Full refund |
| **(new)** Date, venue or format changed by us | Keep your slot for the new date or venue, or withdraw within 7 days of our notice for a full refund |
| **(new)** Start time changed on the same day, or a match moved to another court at the same venue | No refund; the event continues as planned |
| **(new)** Event stopped after it has started, for safety, weather, government orders, unrest or other reasons outside our control | The tournament committee decides results from matches played. If fewer than half of the scheduled matches were played and the event is not completed later, teams receive a 50% refund |
| **(new)** Your team cannot reach the venue because of a general strike, hartal or government restriction on the event day | Treated the same as the event being stopped, if we postpone; otherwise no refund |

**How refunds are paid**

1. **(new)** Refunds are sent by bKash to the number the payment came from, within 14 days of being approved.
2. **(new)** bKash charges for sending the payment are not refunded.
3. Refunds go to the team, not to individual players. Sharing a refund among players is the captain's responsibility.

**Changes we tell you about**

We announce changes by email to captains and players, in the website account, and on our official Instagram @obsidiancup and Facebook page. Captains must make sure their players know.

**Open question:** should the policy also offer a credit for the next event instead of a refund? It helps keep money in the tournament, but it must stay optional.

The guardian opens it on their own phone, fills in their details, ticks each box and types their full name as a signature. The link expires after 7 days or once used. A paper copy of the same form is kept at check-in for guardians who cannot use the link.

## 7. Captain Code Terms and Captain Declaration

The code terms are shown when a captain enters a code. The declaration is agreed when the captain submits the team's registration.

**Captain Code Terms — version 1.0**

1. Captain codes are given only by Obsidian Cup, by request to obsidiancup.official@gmail.com or our official Instagram.
2. Each code works once, for one team in one event, and expires on the date shown with it.
3. The code becomes tied to the account that uses it first. Do not share it. If someone else uses your code, contact us immediately and we will cancel it.
4. Obsidian Cup may refuse or cancel a code at any time, for example if a category is full or the request appears false.
5. Captaincy can be moved to another player in the team only by Obsidian Cup, on request.

**Captain Declaration — version 1.0**

As captain of the team named in this registration, I confirm that:

1. I am authorised by my team to register it and to act for it with Obsidian Cup.
2. Every player on the roster joined the team themselves through their own account. I have not created accounts for anyone else or registered any player without their knowledge.
3. To my knowledge, every player meets the eligibility requirements of this category.
4. I will pay the team fee as set out in the Payment, Refund and Event Change Policy, which I have read and agree to, and the transaction ID I enter is genuine.
5. I will pass on all announcements from Obsidian Cup to my players and make sure the team follows the Event rules and the code of conduct.
6. I understand that false information from me may lead to the disqualification of the whole team without refund.
7. My agreement is recorded electronically with the date, time and version, and counts as my signature.
