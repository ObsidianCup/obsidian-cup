Tournament rules

Volleyball rules from our latest season. Every event can add its own details, which we publish with its registration.

  1. Eligibility

    All participating players must meet the eligibility criteria specified for the tournament category listed on the registration page for that event (for example, age limits for interschool or U19 categories). Team managers or captains are responsible for ensuring all players are eligible.

  2. Team composition

    Teams must register a minimum of 6 and a maximum of 12 players, with up to 2 liberos. A valid team roster must be submitted during registration with identification documents for each player.

  3. Identification

    Each player must provide a valid scanned copy of one of the following: National ID Card, Passport, or current institution Student ID. Failure to provide valid identification will result in disqualification of the player or team.

  4. Match rules

    All matches will follow standard FIVB volleyball rules unless otherwise specified. The tournament committee reserves the right to modify rules for specific rounds and will communicate any changes in advance.

  5. Conduct

    All players, coaches, and team representatives must conduct themselves with respect and sportsmanship at all times. Any form of unsportsmanlike conduct, verbal abuse, or physical altercation will result in immediate disqualification.

  6. Punctuality

    Teams must be present at the venue at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match. Failure to appear within 15 minutes of the scheduled match time will result in a forfeit.

  7. Registration and payment

    Registration is only confirmed after payment via bKash is received and verified by the tournament committee. Slots are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Teams must pay within 48 hours of submitting their registration, or the slot will be released. Once payment is verified, we confirm the slot and issue an invoice within 3 days.

  8. Photography and media

    By participating in Obsidian Cup, players and teams consent to being photographed and filmed during the tournament. Media may be used for promotional purposes on official Obsidian Cup channels.

  9. Committee decision

    The tournament committee's decisions on all matters are final. Any disputes must be raised formally through the official team representative and will be addressed by the committee.

  10. Changes and updates

    Obsidian Cup reserves the right to make changes to the schedule, venue, or rules. All registered teams will be notified via their registered contact information and through the official Instagram @obsidian.volleyball.
