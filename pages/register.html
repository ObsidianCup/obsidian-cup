How Season 5 registration will work

Every player makes an account

Players sign up on this website and fill in their profile once. It is kept for every season after that.

The captain builds the team

Any player with a confirmed account can create a team and becomes its captain. Players join with the team's link.

Pay and get confirmed

The captain pays the entry fee by bKash and enters the transaction ID. We check it and confirm the team's slot.

A slot is confirmed only after payment is checked. Players under 18 also need a parent or guardian to give consent before they can play.
