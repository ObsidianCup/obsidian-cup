Our story

About Obsidian Cup

A genuine, wholehearted try at giving sports in Bangladesh the experience it deserves.

Obsidian Cup is a try. A genuine, wholehearted try at giving sports in Bangladesh the experience it actually deserves. Better venues, real production, and a standard of care that players can feel from the moment they step onto the field or court.

Behind it is Uriana, Tafhim, Masheer, Rifa, Nabid, Nazia, and Talha: a small circle that keeps showing up for the game, the players, and the details that make a tournament feel cared for.

We want this magic to spread. The feeling of a well-run tournament, of a crowd that's alive, of walking off knowing the experience was worth everything. That feeling belongs to everyone. Futsal has now joined the story, volleyball keeps growing, and every season teaches us how to build the next one with more care.

This isn't just a tournament. This is our air. We breathe it, we build it, we give it to everyone who steps on that court.

Where this is going

Volleyball was the beginning. But sport in Bangladesh deserves a real home across the board, not just one court. We want to help build that home for every sport, every player, and every person who shows up ready to belong.

We are working toward a proper platform for our athletes: a space where those who burn with passion get the chance to show it, where dedication is seen and effort is recognised. In Bangladesh, most athletes never get that moment. We want to change that, slowly, intentionally, and with real care, starting with the courts we already know.

Every athlete deserves a stage. We're building it.
