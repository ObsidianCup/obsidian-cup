The court where Obsidian started. Four events since January 2026, latest first, with 43 teams and support from the Bangladesh Volleyball Federation.

How it started

Obsidian started small: a few people who just wanted to play, and a dream that playing could feel like it truly mattered. There was no big plan, only the wish to create the kind of tournament we would want to play in ourselves.

In January 2026 that wish became the first Obsidian Volleyball Cup, with school teams and a girls open-age category at DBox Sports Complex. By July, Season 4 brought 17 teams to the Shaheed Noor Hossain National Volleyball Stadium, with support from the Bangladesh Volleyball Federation.

Every season since has been built the same way, with care for every player who steps on court. If you love this game, wherever you are in Bangladesh, there is a place for you here.
