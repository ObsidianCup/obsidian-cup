About Obsidian Cup

What is Obsidian Cup?

Obsidian Cup is a tournament platform for players from all over Bangladesh. We began with volleyball and have now hosted girls-only futsal through OFC. Every event is built to give players a proper, well-run competitive experience: good venues, real production, a great atmosphere and genuine care.

Who runs Obsidian Cup?

Obsidian Cup is organised by Uriana, Tafhim, Masheer, Rifa, Nabid, Nazia, and Talha: a small circle of people who love the game and are committed to doing it right.

How many seasons have you run?

Five events so far: OVC Season 1, OVASC Season 1, OVSC Season 2, the girls-only Obsidian Futsal Cup Season 1 on 23 June 2026, and Obsidian Volleyball Cup Season 4.

What sports do you cover?

Volleyball is where we started, and futsal has now joined the story. More sports, more athletes, and more carefully run tournament stages are part of the plan.

Registration and payment

Does filling the registration form confirm my team's spot?

No. A team's slot is confirmed only after the entry fee is paid and we have checked the payment. From Season 5, the captain enters the bKash transaction ID in the team's registration on this website and we confirm the slot there.

What happens if we pay but don't enter the transaction ID?

Your slot is not confirmed until the transaction ID is entered. Without it we cannot match the payment to your team, so enter it as soon as you pay.

What is the refund policy?

If your team cannot participate, send a written cancellation via email at least 24 hours before the event and we will issue a 50% refund. No refund is issued after this window.

Can we change a player after registering?

Before the registration deadline, yes: contact us via Instagram, Facebook, or email. After the deadline, changes are at our discretion and decided case by case.

Events and match day

Is there an entry fee for spectators?

No. All Obsidian Cup events are free for spectators. Come and support.

Will photos and videos be taken at events?

Yes. All Obsidian Cup events are photographed and filmed. By participating or attending, players and teams consent to their images and videos being used for documentation and promotion across our social media channels.

What happens if an event is cancelled or the venue changes?

We will notify all registered teams immediately via email and social media. If the event cannot be rescheduled, a full refund will be issued.
